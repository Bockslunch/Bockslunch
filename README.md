# Christopher Taylor

**Data Scientist @ TUMI Genomics** · MS Data Science candidate, CU Boulder

I build the connective tissue between systems that were never designed to talk to each other.

At TUMI Genomics — a cannabis pathogen testing lab — customer data lived in three places with no shared identity between them: a CRM, a custom LIMS lab platform, and an e-commerce store. I built the unified customer database and persistent ID layer that resolved it, then the attribution, scoring, and tiering models that turned it into something the sales team uses every morning.

Before this I spent ten years in molecular and cellular biology research — ALS mechanisms in iPSCs at Northwestern, *Chlamydia* pathogenesis at Midwestern, immunocompromised murine models at CU Anschutz, and GMP analytical development for cell and gene therapy at AGC Biologics. That background is why I ended up doing data work at a genomics company rather than in spite of it: I can read the lab's data because I've generated it.

---

### What I work on

**Entity resolution & data integration** — fuzzy matching, multi-pass reconciliation pipelines, persistent identity layers across systems that disagree with each other

**Data quality at scale** — automated profiling, completeness monitoring, regression detection against baselines

**Applied analytics** — customer segmentation, scoring models, revenue attribution

**Interests** — machine learning and deep learning, particularly computer vision and LLMs. Open to collaborating on nearly anything; variety is how I got here.
